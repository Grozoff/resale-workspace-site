Shopa

A private Windows desktop app for a one-person resale business: storage units, pallets and other purchases, the items found in them, marketplace listings, sales and results.

What it does

  • Records purchases with their invoices and receipts, and the items found in them with photos.
  • Prepares listing text for marketplaces, records sales, refunds, expenses, disposal and donations, and reports the results.
  • Keeps everything on your own computer. Photos and documents can be stored in a folder you choose in your own Google Drive.

Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, the app asks to see your email address (to show which account is connected) and for access to only the files and folders it creates itself (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). It does not read or change your other Drive files.
