Privacy policy

Shopa · effective October 12, 2026

Shopa is a Windows desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no server and no user accounts of its own. Its developer does not receive, collect or store your data.

Data the app keeps

Your purchases, items, listings, sales, expenses and settings are stored in a database on your computer. Photos, invoices and receipts are stored either on your computer or in a folder you choose in your own Google Drive. Backups you create are stored in the same places.

Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, the app requests:

  • your email address (email, openid), only to show which Google account is connected;
  • access to the files and folders the app itself creates (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file), to save, read, move and back up your photos, documents and backups there.

Only if you explicitly allow access to files saved by an earlier version of the app does it request broader Drive access to reconnect them; it still works only with its own folder and files.

The app does not read or change your other Drive files. Google user data is used only to provide these features to you. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not transferred to the developer or to anyone else, except as described under AI features when you start one of them. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your computer (Windows data protection) and are sent only to Google.

AI features (optional)

If you start listing text generation or "Fill from documents", the photos, notes and document pages you choose are sent to the AI service you have signed in to on your computer (OpenAI Codex or Anthropic Claude) under your own account, and that service's terms and privacy policy apply. Nothing is sent unless you start such a feature.

No tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send usage data anywhere.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Drive in the app's Settings at any time. You can also remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete items, purchases and documents, or delete all data, in the app. Files the app created in your Drive remain under your control in your Drive.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. For questions, contact the developer through github.com/Grozoff.
